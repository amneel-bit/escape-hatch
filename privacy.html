Prototype privacy notice

Your project stays in your browser.

This prototype has no server-side upload, account, payment, analytics, advertising, or email collection.

Project files

Selected folders and ZIP files are processed locally by JavaScript running in your browser. The prototype does not transmit project contents, filenames, environment keys, reports, or secret values to a server.

Local storage

If you click the beta button or save feedback, the time and message are stored only in this browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes them. They are not transmitted.

Limitations

A browser extension, analytics endpoint, waitlist, or payment system may be added in a later version. This notice must be updated before any such collection begins, and the interface must disclose transmission before it happens.

Safety

Do not send project archives or secret values to anyone claiming to provide support. Remove .env and secret files from archives, and rotate credentials if they may have been exposed.
